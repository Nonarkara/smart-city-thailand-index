5. Key Government and International Sources

  • NSO — National Statistical Office (demographics, household assets, labor)
  • NESDC — National Economic and Social Development Council (GDP, digital economy targets)
  • GISTDA — satellite flood-frequency data blended into livability scores
  • PCD — Pollution Control Department air-quality signals
  • RTP — Royal Thai Police road-fatality data blended into safety scores
  • MOPH — Ministry of Public Health hospital/health indicators
  • BOI — investment incentives and EEC committed investment figures
  • depa — Smart City Thailand certification and operational status
  • World Bank CCDR 2025 — climate investment needs and flood-risk projections
  • ASEAN Smart City Financing Toolkit / ADB ACGF / UNCDF — financing frameworks

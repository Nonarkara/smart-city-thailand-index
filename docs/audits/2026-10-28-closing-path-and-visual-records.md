# Closing-path geometry and cultural records — 28 October 2026

## The task

A reader should finish a page with the source limits, reuse terms and next action together. A city dossier should offer a real, attributable glimpse of place and history, with an explicit distinction between a provincial reference and a project site.

## Cultural coverage and provenance

There are 93 visual records associated with all 77 provinces. Every one of the 118 dossiers resolves to a verified nonregional first record. This is provincial visual coverage; it does not prove a monument lies inside a particular smart-city boundary. Two Met objects remain explicitly regional comparisons with the original attribution uncertainty.

The collection includes temple sculpture, murals, manuscript libraries, a pottery-making museum diorama, local museums, architecture and a historical Trat press illustration. Captions distinguish an illustration from a photograph, a museum reconstruction from an ancient person, and the source-attributed publication date from a depicted event date. No unsupported construction dates were added to fill gaps.

All records carry source links, author/credit and reuse terms. There are 85 new or upgraded uncropped JPEG/WebP pairs and eight retained WebP assets: 178 real local images checked for format and dimensions. Reuse rights are per image; openly licensed does not mean attribution or share-alike conditions disappear. The source register exposes the actual records and city mappings at `/heritage/source-register.json`. Its generator writes public and dist deterministically at postbuild.

Metadata and visual inspection matter separately. Commons filenames were misleading in several cases: a supposed stupa showed a wooden hall, a temple façade record showed a reclining Buddha, and a procession label showed fireworks. Those captions or source choices were corrected after inspection. Contact sheets and risky individual records were reviewed independently. Full source metadata for the existing Commons and Met records was also rechecked.

Documented dossier hero stand-ins, including Khon Kaen imagery for Wangchan Valley and an overseas event photo for a municipal profile, now use verified provincial cultural context with subject, scope, author and rights visible. Authored city photographs remain. Other photo-registry placements have not received a universal documentary-location clearance.

## Data honesty and limits

See [the data claims audit](2026-10-28-data-claims-reaudit.md) for exact corrections and primary references. Seed observation periods are no longer fabricated as 2025; raw values remain available as unverified seed estimates where appropriate. Overview, comparison and printed-canvas highlights no longer present unsupported service claims as proof of operation. Financial heuristics do not establish credit quality, financing eligibility or actual FDI disbursement. Privacy copy reflects the configured IP/browser/location/referrer data flow rather than claiming anonymity or no sharing.

All city scores and raw values were preserved. This work does not certify every legacy numerical claim, live service capacity, current investment terms or the source of every old coordinate. Maps retain their interaction and now identify legacy reference points as unverified project locations/boundaries. A geometry, build or source-register pass is not a blanket data-truth certificate.

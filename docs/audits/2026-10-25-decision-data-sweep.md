## Corrected mechanisms

- Purpose lenses previously added hidden metric bonuses: low GPP was rewarded as cheap living, a hard-coded university list earned startup points, and missing annual PM2.5 was treated as30. All lenses now use only their displayed pillar weights. Existing IDs remain; learning and visiting lenses identify what is and is not measured.
- City matcher previously awarded surplus threshold bonuses then capped at100%, allowing cities that missed one criterion to be called perfect matches. It now reports an actual weighted research score out of100. High/medium/no preference has weight3/2/0. No preferences uses the published composite.

Displayed chip values now come from the current city record. Text identifies specific checks; annual air does not establish climate safety, GPP does not measure wealth, and digital scores do not prove operating services.
- News footer repeated an obsolete48city/23province/420signal/6verified-source snapshot. Counts now derive from the current registry and methodology constants; source families are not labelled verified. Selected articles are dated rather than advertised as weekly updates. Framework references no longer imply ISO certification.
- EEC inference used the entire east region. A shared province-membership function restricts EEC context to Chachoengsao, Chonburi and Rayong (accepts Chon Buri spelling). Applied to investor pitches, city analytics, command-center tags, financing toolkit and SWOT.
- Economy score no longer establishes BOI eligibility; EEC membership is an eligibility-check prompt, not automatic tax relief. Replaced automatic8year and fixed5year promotion labels with activity/certificate qualification.
- Moneyball analytics no longer fabricate missing air/crime/green/bed values, infer rent and wage discounts from low GPP, or infer operation and market saturation from digital/hospitality scores.

## Limits

This corrects presentation and inference; it does not independently validate every curated seed value. Provincial figures remain different from municipal observations. Costs, rent, tuition, admission, job vacancies, visa/work permission and project-specific incentives require direct verification. Legacy financing advice elsewhere still includes indicative project sizes, bond-market access and proposed instruments; these are curated ideas, not verified financing offers. The site cannot honestly be described as entirely real-time or investment-grade solely because the release tests pass.

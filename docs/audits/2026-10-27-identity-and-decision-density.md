# Identity and decision density — 27 October 2026

The task is to compare places and reach their evidence without paying for repeated identity or traversing every city story. A city name and score dominate each shortlist row. A province subtitle earns a line only when it adds geographic context.

## Diagnosis and changes

- Home editorial lists repeated Phuket / Phuket and Yala / Yala, among others. A shared localized helper now suppresses that identity subtitle across home, rankings, discovery, comparison pickers, baseline identity rows, investment cards and mini-map labels. Korat / Nakhon Ratchasima, Rattanakosin / Bangkok and Koh Samui / Surat Thani remain. Exports, schemas, search geography, explicit provincial evidence qualifiers and comparison matrix province rows are preserved.
- Short home records measured 54px before. Single-line records now use 44px with 14px names; a full distinct province or long name can earn a second line. Removed outlined padded cards, retained all 14 editorial entries and their scores. Columns align to their own content rather than stretching to the longest sibling.
- The air list claimed clean air against an unspecified WHO interim target without an actual threshold-based selection. The fixed list is now explicitly editorial; rail funding, investment inflow and national outperformance claims were also removed. Each group identifies its displayed values as curated composite scores out of 100. These corrections do not change city scores or pretend a new air measurement was collected.
- About, creative economy and why pages carried 112px obsolete header offsets after a shared photo header. Removing those offsets moved titles up 112px at 390 and 1440. The invalid-city error lost 96px of obsolete offset. ASUS lost its 72px offset and its fixed desktop row tracks now recompose on phones: the city-name column previously had only about 41px on a phone; it now has about 163px. The observed ASUS page overflow was traced to the shared header, not attributed to the row grid.
- The shared correction form occupied about 399px by default. A native disclosure now uses about 69px closed. All form fields, keyboard operation, submission behavior, resizing and draft retention remain available when opened; no real message was sent during verification.
- The map controls could not wrap, producing 53px phone overflow. Controls now wrap, grid tracks allow shrinking, and header padding is valid below the content-column width. The shared topbar had the same negative-padding defect at intermediate widths; its actions now retain their width while the wordmark can wrap.
- Rankings exposes the same purpose choices in one native selector on phones, retaining the desktop tabs and weights disclosure.
- The investment atlas originally mounted 118 result cards plus four expanded featured stories. Search and 12-result pages preserve access to all 118 cities; the four complete featured stories are in a separate optional disclosure. Filters precede results. The East filter no longer implies every eastern province belongs to the EEC. A sort based on research-tag count is labeled as such rather than claiming measured investability. Measured phone atlas section: 42,725px → 4,369px; controls 3,976px → 221px from its start. Desktop: 15,715px → 1,759px. These are section measurements, not a claim that the entire site was shortened by that percentage.

## MSH and regression coverage

The shared MSH documents, workspace instructions and both installed anti-slop skills now explicitly classify redundant identity as SH. The rule preserves distinct geography and source scope. A row-height pass does not clear duplication; a duplicate-label check does not clear padding, clipping or separated actions.

`identity-density.mjs` measures all 14 home editorial entries in EN/TH/CN at 390×844, 730×900, 1024×900, 1280×720, 1440×900 and 1980×900. It verifies full labels, distinct geography, readable names, bounded rows/sections, overflow, keyboard city navigation and the correction form. Local mutations restore a duplicate province, inflated rows and nonwrapping map controls and must be rejected. It also checks the repaired intro routes at 390/1024/1440.

The existing investor density gate now accepts omission of semantically redundant province subtitles while requiring distinct geography and every score/fact. ASUS and atlas have separate rendered checks. CI and CDPT run these compiled-build guards alongside tests, lint, build and the existing whole-route type/rhythm gate.

## Coverage and limits

Independent review checked the home rows and correction disclosure.

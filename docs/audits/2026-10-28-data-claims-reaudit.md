# Data and claims re-audit — 28 October 2026

This pass tests the stored research model and corrects demonstrable interpretation errors. It does not certify every external claim, current service or investment opportunity in 118 cities.

## Verified and corrected

- **All 118 seed records:** city-CDP observation rows previously assigned every raw metric an asserted “2025 baseline” or “2025 annual average,” despite the existing provenance contract explicitly saying their timestamps are editorial defaults. The row period now states that the original observation period is not independently verified. Legacy timestamp fields are retained for compatibility, with their existing provenance warning. Numeric values, city scores and the frozen baseline snapshot are unchanged.
- **Financial interpretation:** curated city proxies were presented as revenue, institutional capacity, private investor interest and financing readiness. The dossier now calls them research finance screens/proxies and states what they cannot establish. An immediately repeated quick-fact score was removed; the decision brief and full method remain. Legacy toolkit recommendations no longer promise debt-service capacity, commercial access or THB5B projects from a heuristic score.
- **BOI/EEC:** location does not establish promotion eligibility. Toolkit text now requires project/activity/location-specific confirmation. Unsupported example tax-savings ranges and infrastructure guarantees were removed. The Shah Alam example contained a 74 total alongside 25+50 funding components; it now identifies that reconciliation is needed rather than publishing contradictory arithmetic.
- **Governance:** Invest and Knowledge no longer assert that the PAO universally controls the money, the governor signs every project, or speaking to either office secures finance/permission. They identify distinct institutions and project-specific budget, procurement, land and regulatory responsibilities. The unsupported universal two-year-funding/four-year-delivery timetable was removed.
- **Capability claims:** About no longer claims complete comparable FDI, labour-cost and capacity datasets. Creative Economy no longer claims a formal CEA/TCDN support assignment or complete UNESCO reporting service. The nine Creative Cities count is explicitly tied to the 2025 additions rather than an undated regional superlative.
- **Showcase:** 131,908 LINE friends is attributed to municipal case material and distinguished from residents, active users and alert reach. Unsupported “20% at best” comparisons, “most reliable” assertions and a “70 million Thai users” claim were removed. The national LINE comparison is now LY Corporation's dated 54 million figure for December 2024. A 48-hour statement is a reported target, not a platform guarantee. Wat Phra Mahathat's outdated tentative-list claim is corrected to its 2026 inscription.
- **Generated SWOT:** research scores are no longer called proven service performance or critical infrastructure gaps. Missing data does not produce an all-clear. Annual PM2.5 uses WHO2021's5µg/m³ annual guideline, with a stored-reading qualification. Region-only tourism/security claims, automatic green-bond/BOI eligibility and tier-upgrade promises were removed. EN/TH/CN share this policy.
- **Printable canvas:** localized identity, conditional province subtitle, curated /100 label, seed cutoff, dossier/source and frozen-baseline links. Raw periods and local geographic boundaries are explicitly unverified in the worksheet. Missing values display a dash; genuine numeric zeros are retained. All four workshop boxes, blank writing areas and checklists remain.

## Verification and remaining limits

Twelve core research/data test files passed 89 tests, including scoring, dimension scales, source-baseline calculations, province environment/narratives, snapshots, city-CDP exports, decision weights and EEC geography. Targeted follow-up tests cover all 118 seed period labels, finance scope, missing metrics, multilingual SWOT, dated LINE/UNESCO claims and printable-canvas zeros/identity. Scoped ESLint and a TypeScript build passed; final combined CI and live release remain the parent's responsibility.

Still unverified: every individual city narrative's original supporting document; municipality-reported showcase outcomes whose source PDFs are named but not linked; complete row-level observation periods for curated seeds; current land prices, actual FDI disbursements, infrastructure capacity, borrower accounts, lender decisions and grant availability. Existing archived Moneyball factsheets and legacy narrative claims need their own source-by-source reconciliation. Map coordinate registries also lack per-point source/date records in the inspected modules; recognizable city centres are not independently verified project-boundary locations. No coordinates were changed in this audit. A green integrity test confirms reproducibility of stored data, not its external truth or suitability for an investment decision.

# Nong Plub handwriting fonts

Used for Nong Plub narration and attributed provincial notes. Numbers, formal provenance and controls retain the platform typefaces.

- Thai / Latin: Sriracha by Cadson Demak, https://github.com/cadsondemak/sriracha ; source Google Fonts ofl/sriracha/Sriracha-Regular.ttf. SIL OFL, see Sriracha-OFL.txt. Subset Latin, Thai and punctuation; modified internal name SCITI Mascot Thai.
- Chinese: LXGW WenKai Lite Regular v1.530, https://github.com/lxgw/LxgwWenKai-Lite/releases/tag/v1.530 . SIL OFL, see WenKai-OFL.txt. Subset to platform mascot copy; modified internal name SCITI Mascot Chinese.

Converted to WOFF2 using fontTools. When changing Chinese mascot copy, rebuild the subset to include new characters. Original authors retain their copyright; these are modified subsets, not original font releases.

## Owner handwriting · 30 October 2026

Non Architecture Regular, supplied by Non Arkaraprasertkul for Nong Plub’s narration. Copyright: Handwriting by Non Arkaraprasertkul,2026. The original TTF is retained byte-for-byte; WOFF2 is a format conversion with names/outlines preserved. Owner requested this public web embedding; the font is not relicensed under the site's content license. Source checksum and glyph coverage: non-architecture.json.

It is the first face for every narration script. Its77 glyphs cover Latin letters, digits and limited punctuation, with no Thai or CJK glyphs. Sriracha supplies unsupported Thai/punctuation; WenKai supplies Chinese. These fallbacks preserve readable translations without pretending they are the owner's handwriting. Data, formal research qualifications and controls retain their platform faces.
